# Safety Disclaimer

Reloading and ballistics work involve high pressures and inherent risk.
This software is a planning and data management tool, not a safety system.
You are responsible for verifying all data and decisions.

## Key safety principles
- Always follow published load data from reputable sources.
- Start low and work up in small increments.
- Never exceed published maximums.
- Verify component lot changes and re-validate results.
- Inspect brass, primers, and cases for pressure signs.
- Use proper protective equipment and safe range practices.
- Store powder and primers according to manufacturer guidance.

## No guarantee
- Calculations are estimates and may be wrong.
- Sensor data can contain errors or outliers.
- Environmental changes can shift results.

If you are unsure, consult experienced reloaders or a qualified professional.
